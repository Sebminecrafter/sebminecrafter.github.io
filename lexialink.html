How it works

Basically, when you're on Lexia PowerUp, there's a 'logoutURL' tag that follows you around to bring you back to the sign in page you used to open it, and when you logout (or "Return to login"), it tries to navigate to said page as a normal URL, but the developers forgot to guard against javascript injection, as browsers allow you to use 'javascript: ...' in URLs for stuff like bookmarklets or interactable links. When you click "Return to login", the browser runs the custom code you put in here as it should, allowing full replacement or modification of the current page.
